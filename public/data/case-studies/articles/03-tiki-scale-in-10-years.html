We have gone from an ecommerce website on the Magento platform to a large, complex system: built over 700 sets of source code, with 500 operating systems and developed by more than 200 engineers, serving about 400 million customer interactions per month and meeting the operating needs of more than 5,000 TIKI employees and tens of thousands of sellers. During those 10 years, we learned from the first lines of Ecommerce source code to building a complex distributed system. That journey marked the outstanding maturity of the technology platform and the TIKI engineer team.

Over the past 10 years, we have overcome many challenges to build and develop the system to keep up with the company's growth. It's interesting to look back at the whole journey, we see the transformation of the whole system with each step of the company's development. Technology plays a fundamental role, creating a basis for ideas to flourish and creating leaps for the company.

The construction journey of the TIKI system has five major milestones:

  • Building the Talaria platform: Learn how to master building an Ecommerce system.
  • Decentralized architecture: to expand the system to keep up with business growth.
  • Large distributed systems: Build a large system to meet the boom of business.
  • Building infrastructure: Build an infrastructure platform to meet the computing and storage needs of the entire system.

1. Build the Talaria platform.

10 years ago, we started building the tiki.vn website on the Magento platform. We started selling foreign language books, then Vietnamese books, and then opened up all consumer goods industries. 10 years ago, the TIKI engineers team was just a small group of engineers, gathered from many different IT fields. But they all have something in common that is strange to Ecommerce systems. Everything is new, from terms to data structures about products, sales orders, customers... and sales, marketing, and operations processes in Ecommerce in Vietnam. The first 5 years were the time for us to learn from a famous open source Ecommerce platform, Magento. We learn how to install, modify source code, manage databases, design ideas and most of all learn how to operate an Ecommerce system.

From 2014 to early 2015, we began to expand our product line, from only selling books to selling a variety of consumer goods. Traffic is increasing day by day and the Magento platform gradually cannot meet the company's business needs. The website is slow and modifying and developing new features is increasingly difficult. But now, the engineering team also has a thorough understanding of what an Ecommerce system is like. After 5 years of construction, making the decision to demolish the existing foundation was a difficult decision. The engineers, led by Mr. Nguyen Son Tung, gave three arguments to convince the management to change:

  • Magento is a multi-purpose system, difficult to customize to TIKI's own needs and works slowly.
  • To scale, Tiki engineers must extremely understand and master each line of ecommerce code.
  • Mastering the entire platform opens up opportunities to access new technologies, helping create a generation of engineers eager to learn and at the same time attract better people to join.

In mid-2014, we decided to build a new Ecommerce platform named Talaria. Talaria is the name of the winged sandals of Hermes, the god of information. The engineering team is inspired to build a new platform like wings for TIKI to go faster and further.

We started building a new platform with several engineers. Applying Magento's design ideology, we have used it skillfully, removed redundant parts and only selected the designs that best suit TIKI's business needs. At the same time, completely change the architecture of the e-commerce website to be able to withstand many times the load of Magento. Within 6 months, with the efforts and resourcefulness of the engineering team, with only 5 people, the team built a new Ecommerce platform for TIKI. In early 2015, Talaria completely replaced Magento. The new system creates a smooth user experience and is easy to expand and upgrade. Up to now, after 5 years, Talaria is still the main platform serving TIKI customers, its simple architecture facilitates hundreds of engineers to continuously upgrade and develop the system easily. It marks a milestone when the TIKI Engineers team fully masters the development of an Ecommerce system. 2015 also began a new growth period for TIKI.

2. Decentralized architecture to expand business.

During 10 years of development, TIKI business expanded from selling foreign language books to a complex operating system: sales, marketing, transportation... the company from a startup group to a machine operated by more than 5,000 people. The business boom also poses architectural challenges to develop software to meet the needs of TIKI's business expansion. Initially, TIKI's entire operations were built on a single platform, by the same development team. Over time, the business grows bigger and bigger, and products become more and more complex to meet many different business goals. This leads to difficult decision making and development delays as the team size grows and the product expands. Developing on a single platform becomes the bottleneck for expansion for new businesses.

2014 built the ERP operating system. To serve users better, TIKI started building a warehouse system. Having more warehouse operations also entails the need to build an information system for effective management and operation. But Magento is only designed for online sales activities. Engineers decided to build a separate ERP system on the Open ERP open source platform. The introduction of the new ERP system has created a foundation for developing transportation services, the basis for creating the groundbreaking TikiNOW service later.

In 2016 we decided to convert to the Marketplace model. Development must be completed within 3 months. While Talaria and CPN were too cumbersome. Need a space large enough for a new development team to quickly deploy and build a new system. The engineering team changed their design thinking from centralized architecture to Microservice architecture to decompose the system to the smallest possible level. From a large Ecommerce system divided into separate services for products, orders, sellers... Thanks to that, the development process takes place independently and in parallel. With this strategy, the project was completed quickly, meeting the set plan.

Since 2017, decentralization has become the main expansion method for all TIKI: it allows planning, investing resources and developing independent systems, optimized according to each business index. In the following years, a series of new services were born and completed such as Catalog, Fulfillment, Order Management System, Checkout, Order Routing... Within 3 years, the Marketplace system expanded to 100 services, and all TIKI up to 500 services, meeting the diverse operating needs of an Ecommerce platform. It also helps expand the development space for the TIKI engineering team from 60 engineers in 2016 to more than 200 engineers at the present time.

3. Large distributed system.

Within 10 years, the amount of transactions and data skyrocketed according to the growth rate of the business. That makes the system's computational processing more and more complex and challenging. From 2017 to 2020, the number of products increased 60 times, the number of orders increased dozens of times, and the number of visits increased by millions of times per day. This leads to an increasingly complex processing process. Previous monolithic processing architectures did not meet the system's stability and scalability requirements.

Since 2017, TIKI began to transition from a simple web architecture model to more complex distributed architecture models to process data at a much larger scale than before. TIKI engineers have built a stable, reliable and high-performance data integration platform. Data from hundreds of different data sources is circulated and synchronized in real time between all systems. New technologies introduced allow processing thousands of visits per second with a latency of only a few milliseconds (1/1000 second).

Based on new technology platforms, a series of projects of Catalog, Search, Data warehouse, Order. Operation… has been newly built to meet high performance requirements. Currently, every day more than 20 million product information changes are synchronized in real time to all systems, serving more than 4 million product views, 3 million searches, accurately processing hundreds of thousands of order transactions every day, providing business reports for the entire company within a few minutes.

4. Build solid infrastructure.

During 10 years of continuous growth, infrastructure is the department that faces the most challenges and difficulties to meet the company's business expansion needs. In the last 5 years, the infrastructure has only been expanded about 10 times, but has met the company's growth rate of dozens of times. Difficulties in budget and computing resources have forced the TIKI engineering team to exert all their creativity to ensure stability for the entire system.

With a deep understanding of hardware architecture, TIKI engineers operate extremely economically and skillfully to expand the system. From the first days, each server and each component was meticulously considered between performance and price. All components and servers are utilized to their full potential. The infrastructure team classifies needs for meticulous calculation, accurately predicting customer growth to invest in enough new servers, and at the same time skillfully virtualizes old, weak or expired servers to serve the processing below or create an internal testing environment. In many difficult times, the system has to run without backup devices. But thanks to their efforts and deep understanding of the system's infrastructure and characteristics, engineers have ensured the stability of the system with the minimum cost.

In 2016, the infrastructure team virtualized to create a transparent computing platform for the entire engineering team. The TIKI infrastructure team has applied the latest CI/CD process and Kubernetes technology to the entire Engineers block, creating a unified development environment for all applications in the system. Thousands of applications can be brought to production environments quickly and easily at any time. That helps meet the business departments' need for continuous system improvement. TIKI is one of the companies with the largest scale CI/CD process in Vietnam. Every day we have more than 40 new changes implemented, giving users the best and most up-to-date experience.

To meet business needs at the end of 2019, the infrastructure team had to move the entire infrastructure to Google Cloud. It is the fastest transition in the Asia Pacific region. Hundreds of services were moved to the cloud in less than a month without disrupting the entire development process of all teams. That is thanks to the continuous efforts of the infrastructure team since 2016 to build a transparent computing platform for thousands of TIKI applications. Thanks to this timely shift, TIKI infrastructure has met the skyrocketing computing needs of the 2019 sales season. Moving to the cloud is a big milestone in TIKI's infrastructure building process over the past 10 years.

5. Difficult times.

During 10 years of building and expanding the system, TIKI also experienced many difficult moments. Those are the times that the engineering team has to hold their breath to overcome. But like a boxer, each time he endures a blow and endures it, he becomes stronger and more mature.

  • 2013 — Gioc Co Hon crashed the system: This was the first time the engineering team witnessed the ferocity of online shopping. Thousands of customers gathered at the same time to order free items. Processing orders in peak Ecommerce is completely new to the engineering team. This event changed TIKI's system scale design ideology from then on. Until today, scaling to process orders at peak times is always the focus for the TIKI engineer team to constantly optimize.
  • 2015 — Migrating from Magento to Talaria: This is TIKI's first major migration. Talaria replaces Magento to serve customer traffic. At that time, even though we prepared extremely carefully, no one guaranteed it would run correctly. Engineers had to build an extremely complex data synchronization system to maintain both systems at the same time. Over the course of a month, engineers had to deal with countless problems arising from data discrepancies between the two systems. After a month, Talaria finally managed to handle 100% of TIKI's traffic stably.
  • 2017 — Upgrade Marketplace system: Unlike the previous Talaria migration, this time the entire product and order data structure was changed, so the entire system could only be upgraded once to handle 100% of the traffic. The development team must prepare extremely carefully to migrate millions of data records accurately within 5 hours to open for sale before 7am. The migration process was successful. TIKI has converted the whole platform to Marketplace and met a new stage of development.
  • 2018 — First legendary sales season: One month before the first legendary sales season, the system crashed continuously, some days for up to 4 hours, causing heavy damage to the company. The entire leadership team was extremely worried. Throughout September, the engineering team put all their effort into optimizing and upgrading infrastructure, finding and handling bottlenecks. Everyone must ensure that the first day of the Legendary Sales Season — October 10th takes place successfully. At 4:00 a.m. on October 10, engineers completed all final checks. At 9:00 a.m. on October 10, the first sales wave arrived, all the engineers waited with bated breath and the system was stable. The next 3 waves at 12:00 p.m., 3:00 p.m., and 9:00 p.m. all had no problems. And we had a successful sales season for the next 3 months, recording record growth for TIKI.
